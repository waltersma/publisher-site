Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

Aerie Ink ("we", "us", or "our") is an independent publishing imprint operating in the United Kingdom. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. If you have any questions regarding this policy, you can contact us at contact@aerieink.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect

We keep data collection to an absolute minimum. Depending on how you interact with our website (aerieink.co.uk), we may collect the following information:

  • Voluntary Information: If you sign up for updates or email us directly, we collect your email address and any details you voluntarily provide.
  • Technical & Server Logs: Like most websites, our web servers automatically log standard network data when you visit. This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the date/time of your visit. This data is used solely for network security and site performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the minimal data we collect strictly to:

  • Send you news, publication updates, and pre-order announcements (only if you explicitly subscribe).
  • Respond to direct inquiries sent to our contact email address.
  • Ensure site integrity, protect against cyber threats, and maintain basic network security.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help run our site effectively (e.g., website hosting, font delivery via Google Fonts, or email form endpoint handling). These third parties process technical data (such as IP addresses) solely to deliver requested site features securely.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request that we delete your email address or personal details from our records at any time.
  • Right to Unsubscribe: Every promotional email we send will contain an option to opt out of future mailings.

To exercise any of these rights, please send your request to contact@aerieink.co.uk.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect updates to our site features or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
